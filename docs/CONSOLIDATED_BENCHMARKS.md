# Consolidated Dual-V100 128K C2 Benchmark Database

This document aggregates all formal benchmark evaluations, candidate screenings, and hardware telemetry records across **Stages 2 through 10** on the Lenovo ThinkStation P520 (dual Tesla V100 SXM2 16GB, 150W configured power cap, dual independent 131,072-context agents).

- **Repository master CSV dataset:** [`docs/consolidated-benchmarks.csv`](consolidated-benchmarks.csv)
- **Frozen C2 Workload Manifest:** `e413acced27c1991d76ce2b2df195ff73ce2b4f45853b9676e5b9000ef8503ca`
- **Hardware Baseline:** Dual independent agents (GPU 0 / cpuset `0-2,6-8` vs GPU 1 / cpuset `3-5,9-11`), INT8 KV, frozen prompts (~126,967 tokens each), minimum output 1024 tokens.
- **S7-XXS-14..16:** 9/9 scored C2 PASS, three batches each; all IDs closed. [Report](../artifacts/stage7-results-20261101/stage7-iq3xxs-14-16-report.md), [source/build audit](../artifacts/stage7-results-20261101/stage7-iq3xxs-port-source-audit.md). Arms 15/16 use separate 0.1.54-derived port images. Diagnostics are excluded from their scored rows; cache/swap variation prevents causal speedup claims.
- **Stage 8 (`S8-U1`, `S8-C0..C3`, `S8-M1..M2`):** 21/21 scored C2 PASS across 7 arms (three batches each, Strata 0.1.57, source `c3b3f5035506f3e966f1b4b7b188aeaabae92f1d`); all IDs closed. [Continuation review](STAGE8_CONTINUATION_REVIEW.md), [follow-up summary](../artifacts/stage8-followup-results-20261107/summary.md), [U1 report](../artifacts/stage8-results-20261107/stage8-u1-report.md). Unscored instrumented diagnostic `S8-D1` is excluded from scored rows; `S8-M3` (`--kv-resident 40960`) was evaluated analytically without execution; dual-agent 30 tok/s goal not met; page-cache/I-O/swap/output variation prevents causal speedup claims.
- **Stage 9 (`S9-U1`, `S9-D1`, `S9-F1`):** 9/9 scored C2 PASS, three fresh-process dual-agent batches per arm; 9 batch rows + 3 median rows. U1/D1 share upstream 0.1.59; D1 uses verified DFlash2 spec6/conf0.7 without MTP/fallback. F1 is pinned fork 8b4c09b, engine 0.1.45.7, MTP4 and WMMA2=0 with 13 built-in Volta defaults. All IDs closed; preparation/startup-only failures are excluded. [Final review](STAGE9_REVIEW.md), [full-lifetime standalone metrics](../artifacts/stage9-results-20261110/summary.md), [source audit](STAGE9_SOURCE_AUDIT.md). Scored runs used the user-approved **135–1245 MHz** range, differing from historical 1110 MHz runs. Dual-agent 30 tok/s goal not met; no all-metric upgrade, semantic-quality result or promotion; no causal version/drafter speedup claim.
- **Stage 10 (`S10-P0`, `D2`, `D3`, `K1`, `K2`, `K3`, `P1`, `P2`, `P3`, `D4`, `C1`):** 27 scored C2 PASS + 1 diagnostic PASS across 10 arms. Major breakthrough on `S10-D4` (`--spec 8`, conf 0.7) meeting the dual-agent Decode >= 30.0 tok/s goal on both agents simultaneously (Agent A 30.4, Agent B 30.3 tok/s). [Progress & review](STAGE10_PROGRESS.md), [summary](../artifacts/stage10-results-20261110/summary.md).
- **Telemetry convention:** S7-XXS-8..16, Stage 8, Stage 9 and Stage 10 temperature/power rows use 1-second samples across the process lifetime. Stage 8/9/10 standalone reports also record full-process-lifetime means.

## 4. Key Takeaways & Architectural Insights

1. **Throughput Champion:** Two independent **IQ2_XS** servers under **Strata 0.1.47 (Stage 4-C)** remain the absolute speed champions at **995.70 tok/s prefill** and **39.60 tok/s decode**.
2. **Precision / IQ3_S Champion:** Stage 5 **T7-Fixed** (`jmnargi/Strata-V100@2c9b1dd` fork v0.1.45.3 + SIMT + WMMA + SSD=0) achieved **899.23 tok/s prefill** and **21.45 tok/s decode**; upstream 0.1.47 **T4** achieved **887.37 tok/s prefill** and **21.20 tok/s decode**.
3. **Stage 7 Engine 0.1.54 on IQ3_XXS:**
   - Vanilla 0.1.54 achieved **882.45 tok/s prefill median (880.9 / 884.0)** and **24.20 tok/s decode median (24.0 / 24.4)**.
   - Enabling `STRATA_SELECT_SIMT=1` (S7-XXS-2) boosted prefill to **996.55 tok/s median (992.6 / 1000.5, +12.9%)** and decode to **25.0 / 24.6 tok/s**.
   - Earlier WMMA arms showed no established throughput gain (S7-XXS-3: 885.5 / 890.0 tok/s; S7-XXS-4: 988.1 / 994.4 tok/s); those timings alone do not prove dispatch. Later arm-14 diagnostic launches verified generic WMMA execution on this model pack.
   - Disabling SSD stager (`STRATA_STAGER_SSD=0`, S7-XXS-5) yielded identical throughput (~885.4 / 892.0 tok/s) but increased GPU power consumption from 87W to 91W on GPU0 and 92W to 96W on GPU1.
4. **Stage 7 Engine 0.1.54 IQ3_XXS Optimization Flag Screen (S7-XXS-8..12):**
   - `STRATA_SM70_TABLE=1` (S7-XXS-11) was the **only consistent signal**, achieving **−1.4 to −2.0% decode time** on identical-output pairs.
   - `STRATA_SELECT_SIMT=2` (S7-XXS-8) showed no prefill gain (−0.3% vs same-session SIMT=1 control).
   - `STRATA_GDN_REC_WARP=2` (S7-XXS-9) prefill (+0.1–0.25%) was within noise while modifying FP32 summation order.
   - `STRATA_Q8_GATHER_WIDE=0` (S7-XXS-10) showed no measurable decode effect.
   - `--prefill 4096` (S7-XXS-12) regressed prefill by **−36.7%** (TTFT ~126 s → ~200 s) and was stopped after 2 batches.
   - Common-mode shift: all arms showed ~1.2% higher prefill than archived S7-XXS-2, including decode-only switches, confirming cross-date confounders.
5. **Historical Physical Power & Thermal Profile (before Stage 9's 1245 MHz condition):**
   - Sustained decode power is low (~45–75 W) with SM clocks steady at 1110 MHz.
   - Dense 128K prefill draws 120–140 W average with transient spikes up to 166–204 W, causing dynamic clock throttling down to 795–1050 MHz to stay within the 150W TDP envelope.
   - Operating temperatures never exceeded 70 °C, comfortably below V100's 85 °C thermal throttle point.
6. **S7-XXS-14..16 selective ports (9/9 C2 PASS, closed):**
   - Arm 14: prefill **997.6 / 1006.6**, decode **26.4 / 26.2** tok/s. Arm 15 (verified IQ3_XXS WMMA2 port): **961.2 / 969.6**, **26.3 / 26.1**. Arm 16 (+LEAN): **990.8 / 998.7**, **26.4 / 27.0**. All values are per-agent 3-batch medians.
